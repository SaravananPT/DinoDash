⚙️ Game Mechanics
⬆️
Jump System
Single tap jump with squash & stretch animation. Gravity pulls dino back. No double jump — keeps it skill based.
📈
Speed Increase
Speed starts at 5 units/sec. Increases by 0.1/sec continuously. World changes at score 500 and 1500.
🎯
Obstacle Spawning
Random spawn interval 1.5–3 seconds. As speed increases, gap reduces. Never unfair — always survivable.
💥
Collision
Box collider on dino, slightly smaller than sprite for fairness. One hit = dead. Camera shake on death.
🏅
Scoring
Score = distance × speed multiplier. High score saved locally. Daily leaderboard via Google Play Games.
🎁
Coins
Coins spawn mid-air. Collect to unlock skins. Encourages jumping and creates natural risk/reward.
✨ Polish & Game Feel
📳
Haptic Feedback
Light tap on jump. Heavy impact on death. Medium on coin collect.
📷
Camera Shake
0.3s shake on death. 0.1s mini shake on near miss. Builds tension.
🎆
Particles
Dust cloud on landing. Sparks on death. Stars on new high score.
🎵
Audio
Unique music per world. Boing on jump. Crunch on hit. Giggle on revive.

Week 4–5
World 1 — Jungle Complete
Parallax background (3 layers), ground tiles, obstacle prefabs (rocks, cacti, vines), score system working.
Week 6
World 2 & 3 — Ice + Lava
World themes swap at score thresholds. Unique obstacle sets per world. Speed increases between worlds.
Week 7

Banner, Interstitial, Rewarded Video all wired. Test ads running. ReviveButton connected to rewarded callback.
