Game Design Document v2.0
DINO DASH
3D Endless Runner — Temple Run / Subway Surfers Style
Free-to-play mobile game for iOS & Android. Camera follows behind your dino. Swipe to switch lanes, jump, and slide. Built with Unity 6.
iOS + Android Unity 6 · 3D AdMob Monetized Temple Run Style 3 Lanes 3 Worlds Free to Play
🎮 Game Overview
🦕
Character
Baby T-Rex — chubby, bright green, orange belly. Camera follows from behind. Cute 3D model with full animations.
🌋
Story
Tiny dino escapes volcano eruption — runs through 3 worlds: Jungle → Ice Cave → Lava Land. Survive as long as possible.
👆
View
Third-person 3D. Camera locked behind dino at angle — exactly like Temple Run. You see the road coming toward you.
🛣️
Lanes
3 lanes — Left, Center, Right. Obstacles spawn in 1 or 2 lanes. Always at least 1 lane free so it's always fair.
💰
Monetization
Rewarded video to revive dino. Rewarded video to unlock skins. Interstitial every 3rd death. Banner on menus.
📈
Difficulty
Speed starts at 8 units/sec. Increases constantly. World changes at score 500 and 1500. Max speed capped at 30.
🏅
Progression
Collect coins mid-air. Unlock 6 dino skins. Global leaderboard via Google Play Games + Game Center.
✨
Polish
Camera shake on death. Haptic feedback on every action. Particle dust on landing. Unique music per world.

👆 Controls — Swipe Gestures
⬆️
⬅️
🦕
➡️

⬆️
Swipe Up — Jump over obstacles
⬇️
Swipe Down — Slide under obstacles
⬅️
Swipe Left — Move to left lane
➡️
Swipe Right — Move to right lane
Lane positions: Left = X –2.5  |  Center = X 0.0  |  Right = X +2.5  ·  Smooth lerp between lanes at speed 12  ·  Slide duration: 0.8 seconds  ·  Jump force: 10 units  ·  Gravity: –25
🌍 3 Game Worlds
🌴
World 1 — Jungle
Score 0 → 499  ·  Speed 8–14 units/sec
Dense tropical jungle. Bright greens, warm sunlight. Easiest world — introduces mechanics gently. Fog color: sky blue.
🪨 Rocks 🌵 Cacti 🌿 Low Vines (slide) 🍄 Mushrooms 🪵 Logs
🧊
World 2 — Ice Cave
Score 500 → 1499  ·  Speed 14–22 units/sec
Frozen underground cave. Blue and white palette. Icicles fall from above — must slide. Slippery ground feels faster. Fog color: ice blue.
🧊 Icicles (slide) ⛄ Ice Walls ❄️ Snowballs 🕳️ Ice Pits (jump)
🌋
World 3 — Lava Land
Score 1500+  ·  Speed 22–30 units/sec (max)
Apocalyptic. Fiery reds and oranges. Fastest speed. Meteors fall, lava streams cut across lanes. Fog color: red-orange.
☄️ Meteors (jump) 🔥 Fire Walls 🌊 Lava Streams 💎 Obsidian Spikes
⚙️ Game Mechanics
🛣️
3-Lane System
Left (–2.5), Center (0), Right (+2.5). Smooth lerp between lanes. Dino locks to exact position — no drift.
⬆️
Jump
Jump force: 10 · Gravity: –25. Only from ground. Squash on land. Can switch lanes while airborne.
⬇️
Slide
0.8s duration. Collider shrinks to half height. Dino rolls under low obstacles. Can't jump during slide.
🎯
Obstacle Spawning
1 or 2 lanes blocked per spawn. Always 1 lane free. Spawn interval shrinks as speed increases. Never unfair.
🏅
Scoring
Score = distance × speed. Coins add +10 per coin. High score saved locally via PlayerPrefs. Global leaderboard daily.
🪙
Coins
Rows of 3–6 coins appear in a single lane at height 1.2. Spin as they approach. Collect by running through them.
🎥
Camera
Offset: (0, +4, –7). 15° tilt down. Smooth lerp follows dino. Only X axis changes with dino for lane feel.
♻️
Infinite Tiles
7 tiles on screen. Each tile is 20 units long. When a tile exits behind camera it recycles to front instantly.
🌍
World Switch
Score 500 → Ice world. Score 1500 → Lava world. Fog color changes, music swaps, obstacle set changes.
✨ Polish & Game Feel
📳
Haptics
Light tap on jump & lane switch. Heavy rumble on death. Medium buzz on coin collect.
📷
Camera Shake
0.4s shake, magnitude 0.3 on death. Feels like a crash. Snaps back to original position cleanly.
🎆
Particles
Dust burst on landing. Sparks on death hit. Star burst on new high score. Coin sparkle on collect.
🎵
Audio
3 world music tracks. Boing on jump. Slide swoosh. Lane swipe click. Crunch on death. Giggle on revive.
🦕
Animations
Run loop, jump arc, slide roll, die tumble, revive bounce. Squash & stretch on every landing.
⚡
Speed Feel
FOV widens slightly as speed increases. Fog pulls closer. Creates sense of rushing even at same visual speed.
🌈
World Transition
Fog color lerps smoothly between worlds. Sky gradient changes. No hard cuts — seamless transition.
🔢
Score Pop
Score number scales up on new high score milestone. Coin +10 floats and fades up on screen on collect.

📜 All Scripts
🧠
GameManager.cs
Game state machine — Home / Playing / Dead. Controls speed increase and world flow.
👆
SwipeDetector.cs
Detects swipe direction (left/right/up/down) on touch. Also keyboard arrows for testing.
🦕
DinoController.cs
Lane switching, jump (CharacterController), slide (collider resize), death, revive.
🛣️
TileManager.cs
Infinite 3D ground tiles. 7 tiles on screen, recycles oldest to front when passed.
🎥
CameraFollow.cs
Smooth follow camera behind dino. Offset (0, +4, –7), 15° tilt, lerp speed 10.
🪨
ObstacleSpawner.cs
Spawns obstacles across 3 lanes. Always leaves 1 lane free. Interval shrinks with speed.
➡️
ObstacleMover.cs
Moves obstacles toward player on Z axis. Destroys at Z –20 (behind camera).
🪙
CoinSpawner.cs
Spawns rows of 3–6 coins in a random lane at height 1.2. Appears every 3 seconds.
🌀
CoinMover.cs
Spins coins and moves them toward camera. Handles collection trigger and score add.
🌍
WorldManager.cs
Switches worlds at score 500 and 1500. Changes fog color and music per world.
🏅
ScoreManager.cs
Tracks score, high score (PlayerPrefs), coins. Updates HUD text in real time.
📢
AdManager.cs
AdMob setup. Loads and shows Banner, Interstitial, Rewarded Video. Reloads after each show.
🖥️
UIManager.cs
Controls all panels — Home, Game HUD, Game Over. Handles revive/restart/home buttons.
🔊
SoundManager.cs
Plays all SFX (jump, death, coin, swipe, slide, revive) and background music per world.
📷
CameraShake.cs
Coroutine-based shake on death (0.4s, magnitude 0.3). Snaps back cleanly.

📅 10-Week Build Timeline
Week 1–2
Unity 3D Setup + Core Game Loop
Create 3D Unity project. Import all scripts from GitHub. TileManager + DinoController + CameraFollow working. Dino runs forward, swipe to switch lanes, basic jump.
Week 3
Dino 3D Model + All Animations
Source or create baby dino 3D model. Set up Unity Animator with Run, Jump, Slide, Die, Revive clips. Squash & stretch on landing.
Week 4–5
World 1 Jungle — Fully Playable
3 tile types, 4 obstacle prefabs, coin rows, score HUD, death + restart flow. Full game loop playable end to end.
Week 6
World 2 + 3 — Ice & Lava
New tile prefabs and obstacle sets per world. WorldManager triggers switch at score 500 and 1500. Fog and music change seamlessly.
Week 7
AdMob Integration
AdMob plugin imported. Banner, Interstitial, Rewarded Video all wired up with test IDs. ReviveButton connected to rewarded callback. Switch to real ad IDs.
Week 8
Skins + UI Polish + Leaderboard
Skins screen with 6 dino skins. Unlock via coins or rewarded ad. Google Play Games + Game Center leaderboard connected. All UI panels pixel-perfect.
Week 9
Audio + Haptics + Particles + Polish
All SFX + 3 world music tracks. Haptic feedback per action. Particle systems on death, landing, coin collect. Camera shake on death. Score pop animations.
Week 10
QA + Store Assets + Submission
Test on real iPhone + Android device. Record gameplay for store listing. Write descriptions. Upload to App Store Connect + Google Play Console. Submit for review.
